## 1. What "liquidity" means (in one line)
We create a trading pool on **Raydium** by putting in **BZC + USDC together.** People
then buy/sell BZC against that pool. The **USDC is committed, not spent** — it stays in
the pool as the tradable money (and should be locked to prove no "rug pull").

---

## 2. The two decisions

**Decision A — The starting price.** Set by the BZC-to-USDC ratio. It also sets the
token's launch **valuation** (price × total supply of 10.8 B).

**Decision B — The liquidity depth.** How much **USDC** you commit. More USDC = a
steadier, more credible price (harder to move with a single trade).
